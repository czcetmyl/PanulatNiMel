About This Collection

These pages hold the honest words of Mel — unpolished, unafraid, and real. Written in the quiet hours of faith and doubt, joy and grief, ordinary days and glimpses of eternity.

Poetry and prose in Tagalog and English. Not intended for perfection — but for expression. May you find something here that names what you have long felt.
